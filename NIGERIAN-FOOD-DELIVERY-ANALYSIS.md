## 🏆 COMPETITOR ANALYSIS

### 1. Chowdeck (Market Leader)
**Website**: https://chowdeck.com/  
**Tagline**: "You don chow?" (Nigerian pidgin - Have you eaten?)

#### Key Features:
✅ **Multi-category delivery**
- Food (restaurants)
- Groceries (fresh market picks)
- Meds (healthcare supplies)
- Quick-grab items
- Phone chargers & accessories

✅ **User Experience**
- Quick onboarding (3 steps)
- Live updates on orders
- 24/7 support
- Chowdeck Wallet (prepaid)
- Chowscore (loyalty rewards)
- ₦300 off first order promo

✅ **Location Coverage**
- Lagos (Ikeja, Lekki, Surulere, Yaba, Ogudu, Gbagada, etc.)
- Abuja
- Ibadan
- Jos (newly launched)
- Port Harcourt
- 10+ other cities

✅ **Unique Features**
- **Chowpass**: Subscription service
- **Relay**: Business delivery
- **Store vendors**: Restaurants, markets, pharmacies
- **Cuisine search**: "Pasta near me", "Rice near me", etc.
- **Rating system**: Highly rated riders
- **Service fee + Surge fee**: Dynamic pricing

✅ **Popular Restaurants**
- King Glab, Korede Spaghetti, Iyan Aladuke
- Food Fusion, Belefull, Mega Chicken
- Iya Meta, Amoke Oge, Red Pepper
